# Fonts & stroke data

## Fonts (`public/fonts/`)

- `NotoSansDevanagari.ttf` — Noto Sans Devanagari, from [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosansdevanagari)
- `NotoSansSiddham-Regular.ttf` — Noto Sans Siddham, from [google/fonts](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/notosanssiddham)

Both are licensed under the [SIL Open Font License 1.1](https://openfontlicense.org/).

## Stroke data (`src/data/glyphStrokes.ts`)

Generated by `scripts/generate-glyph-strokes.mjs` (`npm run strokes:generate`).
Glyph outlines are extracted from the fonts above, so the animated shape is the
exact letter shown elsewhere in the app.

Brush centrelines are computed from each glyph, not hand-drawn: the outline is
rasterised, thinned to a skeleton (Zhang-Suen), split into strokes at junctions
and corners, then ordered like a hand moves (shirorekha first for Devanagari,
then top-left onwards, always continuing with the nearest stroke). The generator
also reports how much of each glyph's ink the brush passes over.
